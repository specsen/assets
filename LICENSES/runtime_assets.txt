Runtime vegetation pack for RHENUS V28.87
=========================================
This pack contains the GitHub-hosted runtime GLBs used by the widget.
New in V28.87 are two real 3D small deciduous tree assets:
- small_deciduous_tree_real3d_a.glb
- small_deciduous_tree_real3d_b.glb

Poly Haven remains the visual reference/source family for sky and PBR materials
used in the scene. The runtime tree assets in this pack are custom mirrored
runtime assets prepared for the RHENUS widget pipeline.
